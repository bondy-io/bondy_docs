# How-to Guides

Task-oriented directions for writing a WAMP component. They assume you know
what you are trying to achieve and want the steps, not the theory — the
theory is in [Concepts](/wamp/concepts/index).

- [General](/wamp/guides/programming/general) — the conventions every
  component follows, whatever the pattern.
- [Calling and Registering Procedures](/wamp/guides/programming/rpc) —
  register a procedure, call one, and choose an invocation policy.
- [Publishing and Subscribing](/wamp/guides/programming/pub_sub) — publish to
  a topic and subscribe to one, including pattern matching.

The examples run against Bondy, because that is the router we ship. What you
are learning is the protocol; how Bondy itself is configured and operated is
the [Bondy Connect](/router) set.
